# Federation V3 — Business Network / Growth OS expansion

This repository absorbs the modernized content of historical **Part IV, Part X and Part XI**.

## Preserve from the origin concept

- network effects and regional growth;
- franchise templates and local operating units;
- referral mechanics tied to real customer transactions;
- CRM, sales, communication and retention loops;
- AI-assisted churn prediction, funnel optimization and regional expansion;
- path from distribution/trading businesses toward productive businesses;
- social/public communication and participant education.

## Explicitly reject

- recruitment-funded returns;
- treating participant entry fees as operating revenue;
- guaranteed downline income;
- growth models that ignore churn, saturation, quality or unit economics.

## New domain modules

### 1. Business Event Registry
Typed events: lead, qualified lead, offer, sale, refund, service delivery, referral attribution, franchise launch, franchise closure, supplier order, production request.

### 2. Real-value referral engine
Commission is calculated only from a settled external sale/service event.

`ReferralReward = f(verified_external_margin, policy, attribution)`

### 3. Franchise OS
- regional eligibility;
- unit-economics template;
- capital requirements;
- operating checklist;
- brand/evidence requirements;
- local KPI and quality gates.

### 4. Growth system dynamics
Use:
`dN/dt = organic + referrals + franchise_acquisition - churn - capacity_penalty`.

### 5. AI growth layer
- churn scoring;
- funnel anomaly detection;
- personalization proposals;
- regional opportunity ranking;
- franchise candidate ranking;
- all actions routed through `agi_autopilot` policy/capability checks.

### 6. Trader → Producer upgrade pipeline
`real demand → business evidence → upstream production opportunity → synergy_eurasian project → underwriting → funding → production → external sales`.

## Authority boundaries

- business UX/control plane: this repo;
- payment settlement: `synergy_pay_system`;
- accounting/revenue recognition: `synergy_financial_os`;
- productive-project authority: `synergy_eurasian`;
- reasoning: `agi_nexus`;
- operational action orchestration: `agi_autopilot`.
